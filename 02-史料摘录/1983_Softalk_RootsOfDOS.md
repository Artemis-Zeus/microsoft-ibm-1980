# Softalk《The Roots of DOS》——Paterson 1983 年长篇访谈

## 基本信息

- **题名**：David Hunter, "The Roots of DOS", *Softalk for the IBM Personal Computer*, 1983 年 3 月号
- **类型**：期刊访谈（当事人第一人称）
- **形成年代**：1983（交易发生仅一年半后，时点极佳）

## 来源与收藏

- **出处**：原载已停刊的 Softalk；曾挂于 patersontech.com（**该域名现已下线出售**）；现存可靠全文镜像：https://landley.net/history/mirror/interviews/softalk.html
- **查见日期**：2026-09-20（全文核读）

## 内容摘录（关键原句）

- 受雇：「Rod Brock hired me in June 1978, at fifty dollars a day, to make the board work.」
- 写 OS 的动机：「I had always wanted to write my own operating system. I've always hated CP/M and thought I could do it a lot better.」/「I was waiting for Digital to come out with CP/M-86… If they had beat me I wouldn't have taken the trouble.」
- 进度：「By July, he had finished roughly 50 percent of the operating system and called it QDOS 0.10 (for quick and dirty).」/「By the end of August 1980, QDOS 0.11 worked well and was being shipped.」
- 设计方法：「Step one was to write down what CP/M-80 did. Step two was to design a file system that was fast and efficient.」
- 与微软的首笔交易：「They paid us a flat fee. It was not a per-copy royalty, but per OEM. Part of the contract said we couldn't ask them who they were selling it to or planning to sell it to.」
- 得知客户是 IBM：「The first day on the job I walk through the door and 'Hey! It's IBM.'」
- IBM 的干预：「IBM wanted CP/M prompts. It made me throw up.」
- 买断签约：「So on July 27, 1981, the operating system became Microsoft's property. According to the deal, Seattle Computer can still see the source code, but is otherwise just another licensee.」
- 离职：「Then, on April 1, he suddenly quit Microsoft.」（1982，在微软「整十一个月」）
