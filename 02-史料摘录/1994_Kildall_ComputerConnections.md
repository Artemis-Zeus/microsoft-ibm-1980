# Gary Kildall《Computer Connections》自传（1993 草稿 / CHM 2016 公开）

## 基本信息

- **题名**：Gary Kildall, *Computer Connections: People, Places, and Events in the Evolution of the Personal Computer Industry*（1993 圣诞节草稿，未刊）
- **类型**：自传手稿（DRI 一侧唯一的第一人称完整叙述）

## 来源与收藏

- **出处**：家属授权，Computer History Museum 于 2016-08-02 公开**第 1–78 页** PDF：https://d1yx3ys82bpsa0.cloudfront.net/kildall-p.1-78-publishable-lowres.pdf ；发布说明：https://computerhistory.org/blog/in-his-own-words-gary-kildall/ ；IEEE Spectrum 报道（2016-08-03）。
- **查见日期**：2026-09-20（**已全文下载并核读第 1–78 页**；第 78 页之后含 IBM 章的手稿从未公开）

## 内容摘录（官方 PDF 第一手原句）

- DRI 创立：「Digital Research started operation in 1976. It was named 'Intergalactic Digital Research,' because a consultant up in Sausalito held claim to the name 'Digital Research.'」
- Dorothy 的角色：「Dorothy now went by 'Dorothy McEwen,' to avoid an association with a 'Mom and Pop' operation… She contributed in a diligent and directed manner.」
- 公司规模：「In 1978, Digital Research reached $100,000 per month in sales at about 57% pre-tax profit.」（⚠ 同页「到 1980 年已售出数百万份」明显夸大，与 InfoWorld 25 万份口径冲突）
- 论盖茨：「Gates is more an opportunist than a technical type and severely opinionated even when the opinion he holds is absurd.」/「I always kept one hand on my wallet, and the other on my program listings.」

## 未公开 IBM 章（仅经 Evans/BusinessWeek 转引，引用必须注明中介）

- 「飞行故事」之恼：「There is only one problem. I tell this story and, after I am done, the same person says: 'Yeah, but did you go flying and blow IBM off?'」
- 当日实况：「Tom Rolander and I flew back in the early afternoon, to join the IBM discussion. The group consisted of me, the IBM Chess Team, Dorothy, and attorney Gerry Davis… The non-disclosure agreement was signed…」/「We broke from discussions but, nevertheless, handshaking in general agreement on making a deal.」
- 事后：「I repeatedly attempted to contact the Chess Team, headed by Jack Sams. Sams never returned any of my calls.」
- NDA 条款：「All Ideas, Inventions, or other Information become the property of IBM.」
- 1981 对质与价差控诉：「IBM offered to buy out CP/M for its new PC for $250,000.」/「The pricing difference set by IBM killed CP/M-86. I believe, to this day, the entire scenario was contrived by IBM, to garner the existing standard at almost no cost.」

## 提要与备注

- **价值**：推翻「爽约传说」的唯一直接证词；与 Rolander 的独立佐证构成对通行叙事的最大挑战。
- **局限**：① IBM 章未公开，所有 IBM 相关引句隔了 Evans/BW 的转述中介；② 自传在市场规模等处有自我夸大；③ 子女扣留下册的理由（涉及酗酒的章节「不反映他的真实一面」）提示文本可能经过修饰。【CHM16】
