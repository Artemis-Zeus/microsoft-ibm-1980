## 内容摘录（关键原句）

- **盖茨谈钱**：「the primary upside on the deal we had with IBM, because they had a fixed fee er, we got about $80,000… we got some other money for some special work we did er, but no royalty from them. And that's the DOS and Basic as well.」（⚠ 金额口径见争议#2）
- **盖茨谈条款**：「The key to our… the structure of our deal was that IBM had no control over… over our licensing to other people.」/「we were hoping a lot of other people would come along and do compatible machines.」
- **盖茨谈引荐 DRI**（与 Ballmer 互证）：「IBM didn't make it easy. You had to sign all these funny agreements… But so it took a little bit of faith.」
- **Sams 谈 DRI 之日**：「Gary had some other plans and so he said well, Dorothy will see you.」/「We popped out our letter that said please don't tell anybody we're here… And she read it and said and I can't sign this.」/「So we spent the whole day in Pacific Grove debating with them and with our attorneys and her attorneys and everybody else about whether or not she could even talk to us about talking to us, and we left.」
- **Sams 谈 QDOS 授权**：「Gates said: 'Do you want to get [QDOS], or do you want me to?' I said: 'By all means, you get it.'」（与 BW04 版本一致）
- **Cannavino（IBM 高管）复盘**：「the negotiating and the contracts had given most of that control to Microsoft.」
- **旁白**：「IBM never imagined Bill Gates would sell DOS to anyone else.」/ 微软买断 86-DOS 后「to the world for up to fifty dollars per PC」。
- **Allen 谈 SCP**：「And so ended up working out a deal to buy the operating system from him for whatever usage we wanted for fifty thousand dollars.」
