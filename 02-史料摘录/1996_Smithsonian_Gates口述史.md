# Smithsonian 盖茨口述史（约 1996）

## 基本信息

- **题名**："Transcript of a Video History Interview with Mr. William 'Bill' Gates"，Computerworld Smithsonian Awards 口述史项目，美国史博物馆
- **类型**：口述史文字稿（盖茨长篇复盘）

## 来源与收藏

- **出处**：https://americanhistory.si.edu/comphist/gates.htm
- **查见日期**：2026-09-20（HTML 版全文核读）

## 内容摘录（关键原句）

- **运气论**：「The timing was right for the people who got to do it. And that is a lucky thing for them. Certainly, Microsoft got to play that role.」
- **CP/M 谈崩才轮到微软**：「they didn't want to sign the non-disclosure and they really didn't jump on it. So, the project was at risk.」
- **DOS 低价放量策略**：「the one that was low priced, because we didn't insist on significant payments, because we wanted it to get out there, was called the IBM Disk Operating System.」
- **IBM 开放架构的评价**：「by making the bus and the software and everything in here licensable by other people, they setup a thing that the whole industry could rally around as the next generation of 16-bit computing.」
- **兼容机的意义**：「This phenomena that took place right after the PC came out, people built compatible machines, was very important... it created the conditions for an explosion in the software business.」
- **OS/2 之败**：「we had thought that IBM with their corporate presence could really jam it on people… And that proved to be an illusion.」
- **IBM 份额流失**：发布时 65%，五年内滑落至不足 15%（口述中提及）。
