## 内容摘录（关键原句与独有细节）

- Sams 首访：「Sams called Bill Gates on July 21, 1980, asking if he could drop by… 'Don't get too excited, and don't think anything big is about to happen.'」【DA1】
- 二访与引荐：「Having been so favorably impressed with Bill Gates and Microsoft, Jack Sams returned… almost as soon as IBM officially gave Project Chess the green light — on August 21, 1980.」/ 盖茨致电 Kildall：「they were very important customers, 'so treat them right.'」【DA2】
- DRI 谈判结构：「IBM wished to purchase CP/M outright, whereas DRI sought a per-copy royalty payment in order to protect its existing base of business.」/ 证人证言之乱：「Both Sams and Kildall were asked many times in later years about the events of August 22, 1980. Their stories are so factually disparate…」【DA2】
- SCP 协议细节（独有）：初始 $10,000 + 每家 OEM $10,000/含源码 $15,000；隐藏条款全文；「That seemed strange to us, but we agreed to go along.」【DA3】
- 9-30 提案 / 11-06 签约：「On September 30, Gates, Ballmer, and Bob O'Rear flew down to Florida to make their final proposal」/「On November 6, Microsoft and IBM officially signed the contract, which immediately paid Microsoft $700,000 to begin porting…」（⚠ $700,000 与艾伦 $430,000 分账冲突，存疑单源）
- 「One point Gates carefully stipulated: Microsoft would license all of this to IBM, not outright sell it to them, and would expect to be paid on a per-copy royalty basis.」（⚠ 与盖茨 PBS「无版税」说冲突——本库据此立 [争议#2]）
